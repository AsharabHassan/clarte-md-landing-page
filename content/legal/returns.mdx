## 1. The 30-day promise

If the protocol isn't working for you, message us within **30 days of
delivery** and we'll figure it out together. Most cases land in one of
three buckets:

1. **Wrong fit** — we recommended the wrong protocol for your skin.
   We'll swap you to the right one at no cost.
2. **Adverse reaction** — your skin reacted badly to an active. Stop
   using the product, send us photos within 48 hours of the reaction,
   and we'll refund the affected items in full.
3. **You changed your mind** — return unopened products within the
   30-day window for a refund minus a small restocking fee. (See §3.)

## 2. What's not returnable

For hygiene and PMDC labelling reasons, we can't accept returns on:

- Products with a broken seal that have been actively used,
- Single-use sachets or sample sizes,
- Bespoke compounded items (none currently sold, but flagged here for
  future protocols).

## 4. How to start a return

1. Message us on **WhatsApp +92 324 998 6822** with your order number
   and a 1-line reason.
2. We'll send a free courier-collection slot within 2 business days.
3. Pack the items in their original box (or any sturdy parcel) and
   hand them to the courier.
4. Once we receive + inspect, the refund goes back via your original
   payment method, or a bank transfer for COD orders. Refunds clear
   within **7 working days** of inspection.

## 5. Damaged or wrong items

If the parcel arrives damaged or contains the wrong product, take
photos before opening anything else and message us within 48 hours.
We'll replace the affected items free of charge — no return required.

## 6. Adverse reactions

Skincare is personal, and even well-tested actives can cause
reactions in some people. If your skin reacts badly:

- Stop using all products immediately.
- Wash off any residue with a gentle cleanser.
- Take a clear photo of the affected area within 48 hours.
- Message us on WhatsApp with the photo + order number.

We'll refund the offending item(s) in full and our doctor will
suggest an alternative or referral.
