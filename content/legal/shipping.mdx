## 1. Where we ship

We ship anywhere in Pakistan that one of our courier partners covers
(currently **TCS**, **Leopards**, and **M&P** — see §4).

International shipping is not available yet. If you're abroad and
want to send Clarté MD to a relative in Pakistan, place the order
with their delivery address and pay COD on their behalf or via
[PLACEHOLDER — bank transfer instructions once wallet payments are
live].

## 2. Delivery windows

| Region | Window |
|---|---|
| Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad / Rawalpindi | 1–2 working days |
| Other major cities (Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Quetta, etc.) | 2–4 working days |
| Smaller towns | 3–6 working days |

Working days = Monday to Saturday. Orders placed after **3 pm** ship
the next working day.

## 4. Couriers

We pick the fastest courier for your area at the time of dispatch:

- **TCS** — best for Lahore / Karachi / Islamabad metros.
- **Leopards** — good country-wide coverage.
- **M&P** — used when the others can't service the address.

You'll get a tracking number via SMS or WhatsApp once the parcel
leaves our packing facility.

## 5. Cash-on-Delivery (COD)

- COD is the **only** payment method right now. Card and JazzCash /
  Easypaisa will be added once merchant accounts are approved.
- The courier collects the full order amount in cash at the door
  when your parcel arrives.
- **If your order arrives damaged or incorrect**, WhatsApp us within
  **24 hours** at **+92 324 998 6822** with photos of the parcel and
  the affected items. We'll arrange a refund or re-ship.
- Three consecutive failed COD attempts at the same address mean we
  may pause that account until you confirm a working delivery slot.

## 6. Missing parcels

If your tracking hasn't moved for **48 hours** past the expected
window, message us on **WhatsApp +92 324 998 6822** with your order
number. We'll chase the courier and either re-dispatch or refund
within 5 working days.

## 7. Holidays + closures

We don't ship on national public holidays, Sundays, or during planned
inventory closures. Anything announced will be posted at the top of
the homepage at least 3 days in advance.
