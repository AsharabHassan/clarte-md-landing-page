# Augustinus Bader — Bundle / Kit / System Pages

**URLs examined:**
- `https://augustinusbader.com/us/en/the-starter-kit-with-the-rich-cream` — The Starter Kit with The Rich Cream ($220)
- `https://augustinusbader.com/us/en/the-skin-renewal-system` — The Skin Renewal System ($200)
- `https://augustinusbader.com/us/en/the-rich-renewal-trio` — The Rich Renewal Trio ($365)
- `https://augustinusbader.com/us/en/skincare/gifting` — Gifting & Kits hub (14 sets, $42–$1,755)
- `https://augustinusbader.com/us/en/the-evidence-based-skincare-routine` — Editorial routine guide

**What they call it:** Kit / System / Trio / Collection. Never "Bundle." Never "Routine." The word "System" signals clinical intentionality; "Trio" signals curation-as-gift; "Starter Kit" signals a threshold of entry, not a discount.

---

## Hero composition

**The Skin Renewal System** hero reads as a standard single-product PDP: no distinct landing-page hero section. Headline is a plain sans `h1`: *"The Skin Renewal System"*. The sub-copy immediately resolves the customer's question — *"Refresh and renew your skin with three best-selling formulas — The Cream Cleansing Gel, The Essence, The Rich Cream — for a toned, nourished and visibly revitalized complexion."* Products are named inline in the headline sub-copy, not revealed below. No eyebrow text, no lifestyle image. The page opens on the buy-box immediately — consistent with the brand's wider pattern of putting the trust block (evidence, then CTA) at the top.

**The Starter Kit with The Rich Cream** carries an eyebrow: `"Limited Edition"` in small caps. Headline: `"The Starter Kit with The Rich Cream"`. No poetic descriptor line — the name is the descriptor.

**The Gifting & Kits hub** opens with: `"THE GIFT OF SMARTER SKINCARE"` as the page headline, sub: `"Our most coveted, award-winning formulas for face and body — now available in thoughtfully curated gift sets and trial-sized kits."` This is the only Bader bundle surface with a purpose-built hero. The 14 kits below render as a product grid with hero image + name + price + "Quick Shop" + "Learn More" CTAs per card.

---

## Composition display

All three system/kit PDPs use the same **stacked accordion format**: each product in the bundle occupies one card in a vertical list. Each card carries a product image, 3–4 benefit bullets, and a `"View product"` text link to the standalone PDP. Products are *not* read-only — the link is live and explicitly pushes to the individual PDP. This means Bader treats bundle products as dual-registered: they live inside the bundle *and* as standalone catalog items simultaneously.

The **Starter Kit** variant shows products as a **carousel/slider** (4 slides), one per product, with "Previous / Next" navigation. The carousel is the exception; the stacked format is the standard for system-named bundles.

No accordion expand/collapse — each product card is always fully open.

## Pricing transparency

| The Starter Kit (Rich Cream) | $220 | "$231 value" | $11 implied | No |
| The Skin Renewal System | $200 | Not stated | Not calculated | No |
| The Rich Renewal Trio | $365 | Not stated | Not calculated | No |
| Gifting hub | $42–$1,755 | Not stated | Not stated | No |

Bader's pricing transparency is **minimal**. The "$231 value" callout on the Starter Kit is the only savings signal across all pages. The $11 gap between bundle and value is small — Bader does not lead with savings as a purchase driver. No strikethrough pricing, no explicit "% off," no "save $X" pull-quote. The value equation is implicit: you are not buying a discount, you are buying a curated starting system.

The Gifting hub uses "From $180.00" for the Discovery Duo — the only instance of price-range signaling.

## CTA strategy

Buy-box CTA pattern (verified on The Skin Renewal System and The Starter Kit with The Rich Cream):

1. **"Add to Bag"** — primary, full-width button
2. **"Set Up Replenish"** — secondary, same visual prominence as "Add to Bag" (rendered as a peer button, not a sub-link)

The two buttons appear **side-by-side** at buy-box level. "Set Up Replenish" is not an afterthought dropdown — it is a first-class CTA. Per the auto-replenish program page: 20% off eligible products, free standard delivery on every auto-replenish order, minimum 3 cycles before cancel.

A tertiary text link `"View Full Product Details"` appears above the two buttons on The Skin Renewal System.

No per-product "Add to Bag" buttons within the accordion — the bundle is purchased as a single line item. Individual products are browseable via "View product" links but not addable from inside the bundle page.

## Evidence integration

**The Skin Renewal System** bundle page omits clinical stats from the page body. The TFC8® explainer appears mid-page with three unnamed benefit statements and a `"[HOW TFC8® TECHNOLOGY WORKS]"` link. Sample sizes not cited on this page.

**The Rich Renewal Trio** carries product-level stats inside each accordion card (sourced from the standalone PDPs):
- Hydrogel Face Mask: *"92% agree skin feels instantly hydrated"*; *"90% agree skin texture looks and feels smoother"*
- Rich Cream: *"100% agree complexion looks plump and replenished"*; *"95% agree the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles is dramatically improved"*
- Rich Eye Cream: *"97% agree skin looks and feels firmer around the eyes"*

The stats appear **inside the product accordion cards** — between the hero image and the "View product" link — not in a separate trust strip or above the CTA. Position: mid-page, after the buy-box, within the composition display section.

**The Evidence Based Skincare Routine** editorial page carries the highest evidence density: *"95% of participants agreed that their skin looked dewy, radiant, and luminous"* (The Essence), *"97% agreed that skin tone and texture dramatically transformed in 12 weeks"* (The Rich Cream), plus four-week trial references for The Face Oil. These are **inline within narrative text**, not in a 3-up grid format — the copy is the container.

The TFC8® explainer is the constant. It appears at the **bottom** of all bundle pages as a recurring branded module with the "View" link, functioning as a brand-spine anchor rather than a conversion element.

## Cross-sell / upsell

- **"View product"** links within each accordion card push to standalone PDPs — passive cross-sell, not a CTA.
- **"The Club"** loyalty program appears as a buy-box microcopy note: *"earn points"* — retention baked into the purchase moment without interrupting flow.
- **"Deluxe Sample(s) with every purchase over $200"** — GWP (gift with purchase) threshold note appears below the buy box on pages over the $200 threshold. Not auto-added; surfaced as an incentive.
- The Gifting hub cards carry `"Quick Shop"` overlays — a modal add-to-bag that bypasses the system PDP entirely for gift-givers who know what they want.

No cross-sell to other rituals or systems from within a bundle page. Bader does not surface "you might also want The Rich Renewal Trio" on The Skin Renewal System page.

---

## Subscription default

On all bundle PDPs: **"Purchase Once" is the default state** shown (the toggle is on "Purchase Once" when the page loads). The "Set Up Replenish" button sits beside "Add to Bag" as an always-visible alternative — it does not require clicking a dropdown to reveal. The 20% discount for auto-replenish is not displayed as a number on the bundle page itself; it appears only on the dedicated `/skincare/replenish` program page.

Free delivery is the visible replenish benefit on the bundle pages: *"FREE standard delivery on all auto-replenish orders."*

Minimum commitment: 3 delivery cycles before cancellation is possible. This is disclosed in support documentation but not on the product page.

## Voice + visual identity

Bundle page copy matches the rest of the site: authoritative, un-fussy, benefit-led. The headline **is** the product name — no separate poetic line. Benefit bullets are three-to-four words each: *"Gently cleanses and purifies," "Regulates sebum secretion," "Visibly smooths and firms."* Prose is eliminated from the buy-box area; it appears only in the editorial `/the-evidence-based-skincare-routine` guide.

Visual treatment: cream/warm-white background, no protocol-specific color accent, no "system" branding badge on the product imagery. A system is differentiated from a single SKU purely by page structure (the accordion composition), not by visual identity tokens. Sustainability icons (6) appear near the footer area of bundle pages — same position as on single-product PDPs.

Typography: same sans-serif primary as site-wide. No italic Fraunces equivalent. All-caps small labels for eyebrows ("LIMITED EDITION," "THE SCIENCE OF TFC8®"). The TFC8® module heading is the closest analog to a JetBrains Mono eyebrow.

## What to lift for Clarté

- **Stacked accordion per-product with "View product" link.** The product accordion on The Skin Renewal System is the correct structure for Clarté's protocol pages. Each of the 3–5 SKUs in a protocol gets one card: protocol-ordered image + 3 benefit bullets + link to standalone PDP. This lets the protocol page serve as an overview while keeping individual PDPs as the primary trust surface. Apply to `/acne`, `/even-tone`, `/renewal`, `/barrier`.

- **Per-product clinical stat inside the accordion card, not in a separate trust strip.** Bader places the percentage claims *within* the product card in the composition section, not in a separate section above the CTA. The implication: the stat earns its place alongside the product that produced it, not as a page-level assertion. When Clarté has panel data, wire `<ClinicalProof>` output into each protocol-product accordion card, not just into the page's top trust strip.

- **Two peer-level CTAs: "Add Protocol to Cart" + secondary action.** Bader's "Add to Bag" | "Set Up Replenish" side-by-side is the prestige template. For Clarté (COD-only, no subscription), the pair maps to: **"Order Protocol — Rs. {total}"** (primary) | **"Start with the Quiz"** (secondary — routes undecided visitors back to diagnosis rather than losing them). This is a new pattern for Clarté; it lives in the buy-box of each protocol page.

- **"THE EVIDENCE IS IN" all-caps mono eyebrow above the stats block.** This exact eyebrow pattern maps to JetBrains Mono `tracking-[0.18em] uppercase text-xs`. On Clarté protocol pages: `"THE 12-WEEK PROTOCOL · N={N} · WEEK {N} RESULTS"` as the stats-section eyebrow once panel data exists. The Bader copy proves the format reads as clinical, not as marketing.

- **Editorial route as a trust surface parallel to the bundle page.** The `/the-evidence-based-skincare-routine` page is a distinct URL where clinical stats are surfaced as editorial narrative (inline stats within readable paragraphs), not as a buy-box. Clarté's equivalent is a `/how-protocols-work` or `/the-12-week-method` editorial page — it does conversion work without looking like it's trying to. Protocol pages can link to it from the accordion area. \[See: Composition display section above.\]

---

## What to skip

- **"Set Up Replenish" as a first-class CTA.** Bader's 20%-off auto-replenish beside "Add to Bag" is the prestige subscription template — and entirely un-liftable for Clarté. Pakistan has no subscription-billing rails, and a 12-week protocol is a course, not a perpetual replenishment. Showing a "Set Up Replenish" button on a COD-only flow would confuse and undermine trust. Per `feedback_cod_policy`.

- **"Deluxe Sample with purchases over $200" GWP.** Per `feedback_unverified_claims`: no free-shipping threshold, no GWP threshold. The COD-on-arrival model also makes unrequested gifts at the door a support liability.

- **Unnamed clinical stats on bundle pages (no sample size, no duration).** The Skin Renewal System's TFC8® module contains benefit claims without N or methodology. Clarté should not surface stats without methodology — it is specifically the combination of % + N + duration that reads credible and is legally defensible as consumer-agreement survey. Do not lift the bare assertion form.
