# La Roche-Posay — Bundle / Protocol Page Teardown

**Date:** 2026-05-23
**Bundle surface studied:** Exclusive Value Sets collection + individual Routine Set PDPs
**What they call it:** "Routine Set" (most common), also "Value Set," "Kit," "Regimen," "Travel Set"
**Primary URLs:**
- Collection: `laroche-posay.us/offers/exclusive-value-sets`
- PDP examples: `/offers/exclusive-value-sets/mela-b3-dark-spot-routine-set-melab3routineset.html`, `/offers/exclusive-value-sets/effaclar-salicylic-acid-acne-routine-set-EffaclarSalicylicAcidAcneRoutineSet.html`, `/our-products/face/face-serum/anti-aging-routine-set-antiagingskincareset.html`

## URL + page label

Two distinct bundle surfaces:

1. **Exclusive Value Sets collection** — `laroche-posay.us/offers/exclusive-value-sets`. The hub. 15+ sets. Concern-led naming: "Mela B3 Dark Spot Routine Set," "Effaclar Salicylic Acid Acne Routine Set," "Vitamin C 3-Step Radiant Skin Routine Set," "Dry Skin Savers Set," "Anti-Aging Four Serum Set." The word "Routine" appears in most set names; "Set" is universal; "Kit" and "Regimen" appear occasionally.

2. **MyRoutine AI result page** — `laroche-posay.us/routine-finder` → AI selfie → personalized routine output. This is LRP's highest-conviction bundle surface. The result outputs a 3–5 product "Your Routine" recommendation. Called a "Routine," not a kit or bundle.

## Composition display

**Routine Set PDPs** — numbered sequential steps, not a grid or accordion:
- Step 1: [Product name] — [brief benefit line]
- Step 2: [Product name] — [brief benefit line]
- Step 3: [Product name] — [brief benefit line]

Each step links to that product's own PDP 〔confirmed via WebSearch result snippets: "Links to individual product pages within the routine sets"〕. This means the bundle page is as much an editorial/navigation surface as a buy surface — the user can click away to a PDP and buy a single SKU.

The group product image in the gallery shows all products together; there is no accordion expand per product on the bundle PDP.

## Pricing transparency

〔Confirmed via WebSearch result snippets and LovelySkin mirror〕:
- Bundle price shown (e.g., "Mela B3 Dark Spot Routine Set" at $90.92, individual value $106.97 — saves $16.05).
- Sum-of-individuals displayed as the "regular" price; bundle price as the discounted price.
- Savings shown as a dollar amount ("Save $16.05"), not as a percentage.
- Discount range across sets: ~10–15% off the sum of individual retail prices.
- "Exclusive Value Sets" collection framing — the savings are positioned as "exclusive to laroche-posay.us" (not available in stores or on Amazon).
- No fake strikethrough: the "regular price" is the actual combined retail for those SKUs.
- Subscription rails: LRP has a "Replenish & Save" auto-delivery program that gives 10% off + 15% off every third order, but this appears layered on top of, not integrated into, the routine set PDP buy box 〔indirect〕.

## CTA strategy

- Single "Add to Bag" for the full bundle — not per-product Add buttons on the bundle PDP 〔indirect〕.
- Each product in the numbered-step display links to its own PDP if the buyer wants to buy one SKU.
- MyRoutine AI result: per-product "Add to Bag" per routine step — no "Add full routine" single CTA confirmed 〔indirect〕.
- No quiz CTA embedded in the routine set PDPs — quiz is a separate nav entry ("Find Your Routine") not cross-linked from within PDPs 〔indirect〕.

## Evidence integration

〔Indirect — WebSearch result snippets, Hydra case study〕:
- Clinical claims sit in the product descriptions within the numbered-step display, not in a standalone proof block above the CTA.
- Format: ingredient-led ("powered by Melasyl™ and Niacinamide"), not percentage-stat ("X% improvement in Y"). LRP saves the % claims for individual SKU PDPs, not the bundle page.
- "Recommended by 90,000 dermatologists worldwide" — brand-wide claim, not specific to a set. Used on the Essentials Skincare Routine set (confirmed from WebSearch: "recommended by 90,000 dermatologists worldwide").
- No before/after imagery on bundle PDPs (clinical before/after appears on the MyRoutine AI result page as a skin-analysis output, not a static photo pair).
- No N= panel stats on bundle pages — those live on individual SKU PDPs.

## Subscription default

- Routine Sets are one-time by default.
- "Replenish & Save" (LRP's auto-replenish program, 10% + 15% on third order) appears on individual SKU PDPs and may appear on routine set PDPs — could not confirm bundle-specific subscription toggle 〔indirect〕.
- LRP does not default to subscription. The "Replenish & Save" opt-in requires the user to find the toggle.

## Voice + visual identity

The bundle page voice is functional-pharmaceutical — the product line name carries the identity (Effaclar, Mela B3, Toleriane), and the set is positioned as the easiest way to build the right routine for a named concern. Copy is plain ("A 3-step skincare routine for skin discoloration, dark spots, age spots and post-acne marks") — no editorial warmth, no FOMO, no founder voice. The Hydra design system's "60% white, 30% near-black, 10% brand blue" surfaces make the page read as a pharmacy shelf digitized: clean, trusted, and slightly cold. Bundle PDPs don't differentiate visually from individual SKU PDPs — same layout structure, same Avenir type, same white background.

## What to lift for Clarté

1. **Concern-led naming on the collection page** — LRP's Routine Set names lead with the concern, not the product line: "Acne Routine Set," "Dark Spot Routine Set," "Dry Skin Set." Clarté's four protocol pages should follow the same pattern: "Clear-Skin Protocol," "Even-Tone Protocol," "Renewal Protocol," "Barrier Protocol" — never "Bundle A" or "Kit 1." Apply to the protocol page `<h1>` and the product card title in `components/product/ProductCard.tsx`.

2. **Numbered sequential step display inside the bundle PDP** — the step-by-step numbered product list (Step 1 → Step 2 → Step 3) is the right composition for a 12-week protocol. Each step = one SKU, with a one-line benefit and a link to that SKU's own PDP for detail. This keeps the bundle page scannable without hiding information. Adapt for Clarté's protocol pages: "Step 1: Gentle Cleanser — AM + PM" / "Step 2: Active Serum — PM only" / "Step 3: Barrier Moisturiser — AM + PM." Currently the protocol pages (`app/acne/page.tsx` etc.) don't use a numbered product sequence — this is the missing layer.

3. **Dollar-amount savings over percentage** — LRP shows "Save $16.05" not "15% off." For Clarté, the analog is showing the sum of individual product prices minus the bundle price in PKR: "Buy separately: Rs. 8,750 / Protocol Bundle: Rs. 6,499 — You save Rs. 2,251." The absolute-rupee framing reads more concretely for COD buyers who are price-calculating in their head at the door.

4. **MyRoutine AI 3-step selfie flow as the model for Clarté's `/quiz` → AI result** — profile (<1 min) → selfie → "Your Routine" result page with 3–5 SKUs in sequence, each with an "Add to Bag." The crucial pattern: the result page IS the bundle page, not a separate purchase step. When Clarté's AI feature ships, the skin-analysis result should render as a mini-protocol card (eyebrow "YOUR PROTOCOL" / Fraunces italic protocol name / numbered product steps / single "Add Protocol to Cart" CTA). See also the required `/legal/skin-analysis-notice` disclosure (LRP has this page; Clarté must too).

5. **Exclusive-to-site framing for bundle pricing** — LRP positions "Exclusive Value Sets" as available only on laroche-posay.us, not in stores. Clarté's protocol bundles are already DTC-exclusive (COD direct); surface that: "Available only at clartemd.com.pk" on the bundle card and the protocol page hero eyebrow. Converts a constraint into a trust signal.

---

## What to skip

- **Subscription rails on bundle PDPs** — "Replenish & Save" is not portable to COD-only Pakistan. Do not show a subscription toggle on Clarté's protocol pages. The 12-week protocol framing is the natural retention mechanism without a subscription.
- **Per-product "Add to Bag" in the MyRoutine AI result** — if the result page shows per-product CTAs rather than a single "Add Protocol" CTA, it fragments the conversion. Clarté should default to a single "Add Clear-Skin Protocol to Cart" CTA on the result page; per-product links can be secondary/expandable. (LRP's per-product CTA pattern is a concession to their large individual-SKU business; Clarté is protocol-first.)
- **"Recommended by 90,000 dermatologists worldwide" brand-level claim** — this is LRP's "#1 dermatologist-recommended" equivalent. Clarté cannot use a comparable numeric claim without data. Substitute with "Formulated by our GMC-registered doctor" in JetBrains Mono — smaller, honest, specific.
